## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Rogue Eclipse uses arcade-style third-person spaceflight controls. Maneuver with directional input, turn or roll to line up attacks, use the brake to control speed, and activate boost when closing distance or escaping enemy fire. Unlimited Boost is useful during extended dogfights and high-speed approaches.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a traditional character inventory. Between encounters, manage the starfighter's available components, weapons, and modules through the ship customization and upgrade systems. Instant Ship Upgrades can be enabled when applying progression choices.

### World & Environment

The campaign unfolds across multiple roguelike runs and sectors. Battles include starfighters, larger armadas, colossal behemoths, and destructible environments. Completing encounters advances the campaign and reveals more of its space-opera narrative.

### Combat & Weapons

Use the primary weapon for sustained fire and the secondary weapon for heavier or specialized attacks. Boost, braking, rolling, and positioning are central to surviving space engagements. Special abilities and installed modules provide additional combat options, especially against large warships and behemoths.
